Section 01

1. Acceptance of Terms

Welcome to Smash & Spin Sports Arenas. By accessing our website, creating an account, reserving court hours, or participating in tournaments, you agree to be legally bound by these Terms of Service, our Privacy Policy, and our official Court Rules.

If you do not agree to all terms stated herein, you must refrain from booking courts or utilizing our facility services.

Section 02

2. Player Registration & Account Responsibility

To reserve courts and track match statistics, players must register an authenticated account.

  • Accurate Details: You agree to provide true, accurate, and current profile information (full legal name, reachable telephone number, and valid email).
  • Credential Confidentiality: You are solely responsible for maintaining the security of your account password and API tokens.
  • Age Eligibility: Players under the age of 16 must have consent and waiver acknowledgment from a parent or legal guardian prior to court entry.
Section 03

3. Court Reservations, Rates & Payments

All court and table tennis bookings are confirmed in real-time on a first-come, first-served basis upon full upfront payment.

Sport Category Peak Rate (Mon-Fri 5pm-11pm, Weekends) Non-Peak Rate (Mon-Fri 6am-5pm)
Badminton Court $25.00 / hour $18.00 / hour
Table Tennis Table $15.00 / hour $10.00 / hour
VIP Arena Court $40.00 / hour $30.00 / hour

Automated lighting and air ventilation on the booked court will activate exactly at the start of your reserved slot and shut down at the conclusion of the booked hour.

Section 04

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Standard Cancellation Timelines:
  • More than 4 hours before match: 100% full refund or wallet credit with zero processing deduction.
  • Between 2 to 4 hours before match: 50% court fee wallet credit for future rebooking.
  • Less than 2 hours / No-Show: No refunds or credits are granted, as court time cannot be reallocated.

In the event of unforeseen venue maintenance, power failure, or severe weather conditions, Smash & Spin will automatically issue a 100% full credit refund to your player wallet.

Section 05

5. Equipment Rental & Damage Liability

Players renting premium Yonex/Victor badminton rackets, Butterfly carbon table tennis paddles, or ball machines are responsible for returning gear in the condition received.

  • Normal wear and tear (such as expected shuttle feather degradation) is fully covered by the club.
  • Deliberate frame breakage, throwing rackets against court posts, or intentional net tears will incur a replacement fee matching the MSRP of the equipment.
Section 06

6. Code of Conduct & Safety

All players, coaches, and spectators must follow the sportsmanship code:

  • Non-Marking Footwear: Only non-marking athletic shoes are allowed on wooden and synthetic PVC tournament mats. Black-soled outdoor shoes are strictly prohibited.
  • Zero Tolerance: Verbal abuse, aggressive conduct, harassment, or gambling will result in immediate arena expulsion and account suspension.
  • Food & Beverages: Only sealed water and hydration bottles are permitted inside court zones. Food, sodas, and chewing gum are prohibited.
Section 07

7. Limitation of Liability

Athletic sports involve inherent physical exertion and risk of muscle strain, slip, or accidental impact. By booking and playing at Smash & Spin facilities:

Players acknowledge they are physically fit to participate. Smash & Spin, its coaches, and venue management shall not be held liable for personal sports injuries or lost/unattended personal belongings in changing rooms. Lockers are provided for security.
