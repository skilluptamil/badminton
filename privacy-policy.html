Section 01

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, make a court booking, enter a tournament, or contact our customer support team, Smash & Spin collects relevant information necessary to deliver seamless court management and booking services.

Types of Personal Information Collected:
  • Account Profile: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and password credentials.
  • Court Booking Details: Chosen sport (Badminton or Table Tennis), court or table number, reservation date, time slots, racket rental preferences, and playing level.
  • Tournament Registration: Player ranking, doubles partner details, and team division.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and interaction timestamps.

We never collect sensitive biometric data, medical histories, or unauthorized background data.

Section 02

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly to provide court scheduling, customer communications, and platform security.

  • Reservation Fulfillment: Generating real-time court booking confirmations, automated lighting triggers, and digital QR check-in passes.
  • Alerts & Reminders: Sending SMS and email notifications 2 hours prior to your scheduled match slot.
  • Tournament Operations: Organizing brackets, seeding tables, and live score updates.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Preventing double-booking abuse, automated bot spam, and fraudulent chargebacks.
Section 03

3. Payment & Transaction Security

All financial transactions conducted on Smash & Spin are processed through Level-1 PCI-DSS certified payment processors (such as Stripe and PayPal).

Zero Payment Storage: Smash & Spin never stores or logs your full credit/debit card number, CVV code, or banking credentials on our web servers. All payment flows use secure encrypted tokenization.
Section 04

4. Cookies & Web Tracking

We use small data files (cookies) to save your user preferences and maintain active sessions.

Cookie Category Purpose Duration
Essential Session Maintains player login status and active court cart selection Session
Theme & Direction Remembers your Dark/Light mode and LTR/RTL reading preference 1 Year
Analytics Aggregated anonymous performance metrics to optimize court loading speeds 30 Days
Section 05

5. Data Retention & Sharing Policy

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party advertisers. Information is only shared with authorized service partners strictly necessary for arena operations (e.g., SMS dispatch gateways and lighting automation servers).

Inactive account logs and reservation archives are anonymized after 24 months of dormancy unless a legal obligation requires extended retention.

Section 06

6. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, Smash & Spin provides all registered athletes with comprehensive data management rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete digital export of your court reservation history and profile data.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit and update inaccurate profile information anytime from your Player Dashboard.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete permanent deletion of your account and related booking records.
  • Marketing Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from promotional tournament and discount newsletters with 1 click.
