Who We Are

Elevating Racket Sports to Championship Heights

Founded in 2018, Smash & Spin began with a singular goal: to eliminate the hassle of booking quality sports courts and build an inclusive haven for badminton and table tennis players.

Today, we operate a 35,000 sq. ft. dual-sport arena complex equipped with BWF standard shock-absorbent synthetic mats, ITTF approved 25mm tables, shadowless 800-lux LED lighting, and an automated reservation engine that serves thousands of athletes weekly.
