Section 01

1. Check-In & Slot Management

To maintain seamless court turnarounds and avoid delays for fellow athletes:

  • Arrival: Please check in at the reception desk or scan your digital booking QR code from your Player Dashboard 10 minutes prior to your allocated slot.
  • Prompt Departure: When your booked hour concludes and the court timer buzzer signals, please vacate the court promptly so the next group can commence on schedule.
  • No Overstay: Continuing play beyond reserved minutes without extending at reception is strictly prohibited.
Section 02

2. Footwear & Apparel Standards

Strict Footwear Enforcement: Only certified non-marking gum-rubber indoor athletic shoes are permitted on our professional vinyl/hardwood court surfaces. Outdoor running shoes with black carbon soles leave permanent scuffs and are strictly forbidden.
  • Players without proper non-marking footwear can rent sanitized indoor court shoes at reception for $3.00.
  • Appropriate sports attire (t-shirts/jerseys and athletic shorts/skirts) must be worn at all times. Playing shirtless is not permitted.
Section 03

3. Badminton Court Specific Rules

All 6 badminton courts are BWF certified with 1000 Lux glare-free vertical LED lighting:

Player Capacity

Maximum 4 active players on court at any time for doubles, and 2 for singles. Up to 2 resting substitutes on sidelines.

Net Care

Nets are precision-tensioned to standard 1.55m height. Do not pull, lean on, or kick the net posts.

Section 04

4. Table Tennis Arena Rules

Our ITTF-standard table tennis arena features 25mm scratch-resistant tournament tables:

  • Table Surfaces: Do not place sweaty hands, towels, drinks, or heavy bags directly onto the table surface.
  • Barriers & Surrounds: Please maintain 2-meter clearance around barriers to prevent tripping hazards during fast multi-ball rallies.
  • Approved Balls: Only ITTF 3-star 40+ mm plastic competition balls are permitted during match play.
Section 05

5. Equipment Care & Racket Rentals

High-performance graphite rackets and carbon paddles are available for hire:

  • Inspect all rental equipment prior to match play and notify staff immediately of any existing frame cracks.
  • Never clash rackets intentionally or strike the hardwood floor in frustration.
  • Return all rented rackets and shuttlecock tubes to the front desk when your match concludes.
Section 06

6. Hydration, Food & Prohibited Items

PERMITTED Water bottles with spill-proof caps, electrolyte drinks in closed sports flasks.
PROHIBITED Food, snacks, glass containers, chewing gum, alcohol, and any smoking/vaping.

Designated lounge cafe areas in the lobby are available for enjoying snacks, energy bars, and meals before or after games.

Section 07

7. Safety, First Aid & Emergencies

Your health and physical safety are our top priority at all times:

  • First Aid Station: Fully stocked first aid kit, ice packs for sprains, and an Automated External Defibrillator (AED) are located at Reception.
  • Warm-Up Zone: Dedicated stretching mats and foam rollers are provided in the corner gym zone. Always warm up for 5-10 minutes prior to intense play.
  • Emergency Contacts: Alert any court marshal or dial +1 (800) 555-SMASH immediately in case of injury.
